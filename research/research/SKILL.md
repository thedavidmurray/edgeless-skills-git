# Research Skill

## Overview

Use this skill to conduct deep research on topics, technologies, markets, or questions.

## When to Use

- User asks "what is X?" or "how does Y work?"
- Need to evaluate a technology, library, or tool
- Market research for trading or product decisions
- Pre-implementation investigation

## Workflow

1. **Scope**: Define what you are researching and why
2. **Search**: Use web search to find authoritative sources
3. **Synthesize**: Summarize findings with sources
4. **Evaluate**: Assess confidence and note gaps
5. **Report**: Deliver structured output

## Output Format

```markdown
# Research: [Topic]

## Summary
2-3 sentence executive summary.

## Findings
1. **[Point]**: Evidence + source link
2. **...**

## Confidence
- High / Medium / Low per finding

## Gaps
What you could not determine

## Sources
- [Title](url)
```

## Verification

- [ ] At least 3 sources consulted
- [ ] Sources are authoritative (docs, papers, official sources)
- [ ] Conflicting information noted
- [ ] Confidence level stated
